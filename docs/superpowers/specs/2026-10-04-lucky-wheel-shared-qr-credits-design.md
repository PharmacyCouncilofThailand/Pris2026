# PRIS2026 Lucky Wheel — Shared QR Credits Design

**Status:** Confirmed flow recorded on 2026-10-04; two decisions remain open in the final section. This replaces the spin-eligibility and once-per-day portions of `2026-10-03-lucky-wheel-design.md`. It keeps the approved daily Main Session check-in, wheel probability, stock, reward proof, redemption, R2 image storage, and mobile B composition unless a change is stated here. The old implementation plan is historical evidence and must not be executed as a change plan for this flow.

## Goal and fixed user decisions

Admin prepares multiple distinct shared QR codes for a Bangkok event date, initially closed, then opens chosen codes for projection on a large screen. A participant scans a projected QR with the phone's normal camera, follows its PRIS link, logs in if needed, and claims one wheel credit into their own account. Each `(event, QR, account)` can create at most one credit. The same QR can give one credit to every eligible account; five different QR codes can give one account five credits on the same day. There is no daily spin cap. The QR link is not a check-in or an admission QR.

Daily Main Session check-in remains independent and mandatory both when claiming a credit and when spending it. Staff continue scanning the original admission QR through the existing check-in workflow; staff never scan wheel-credit QRs on a participant's behalf. Admin alone manages the QR set, daily windows, opening/closing, claim records and corrections. Existing reward-redemption roles stay unchanged.

Each credit belongs to its QR's Bangkok date. Admin sets **one daily window** for both claiming QR credits and spinning; the start and end are identical for both actions. For example, QR claiming and spinning may both run 09:00–19:00 on one day, with different hours configured for another day. Unspent credits expire at that day's end time and never carry to the next date. An expired or admin-revoked credit cannot be restored by scanning the same QR again. A successful spin remains permanent when attendance is later cancelled; it does not restore credit or stock.

## Current implementation and required boundary

The completed API currently checks today's attendance and `spinByDay` inside `getEligibility` and `createSpin` (`conference-api/src/modules/lucky-wheel/service.ts`). `lucky_wheel_spins` requires attendance evidence and has a unique `(event_id, user_id, play_date)` index (`drizzle/0034_lucky_wheel.sql`). PRIS displays `existingSpin` as a terminal daily result (`Pris2026/src/app/[locale]/lucky-wheel/page.tsx` and `ActivityTicket.tsx`). The backoffice has wheel setup and reward collection but no QR-credit management.

Keep the existing daily attendance tables, policies, scan routes, ticket QR, reports, and historical check-in data. **Do not remove attendance checks from the wheel**: the new rule replaces today's single-play entitlement with a QR credit while retaining registration, Main Session entitlement and active daily attendance as preconditions. `play_date` remains a Bangkok reporting date; it ceases to be a uniqueness key for new spins.

## Day schedule and QR lifecycle

An admin creates one window for each `(event, Bangkok date)`, entering its start and end in `Asia/Bangkok`. Store absolute instants in PostgreSQL and the local date explicitly. The window must lie within that Bangkok date; an exclusive end at the following midnight is permitted. Both claiming and spinning use the **same** `start <= databaseNow < end`, so a 19:00 end stops both at exactly 19:00. Validate `start < end`. Do not infer the window from the Main Session start/end. Show the configured window and warn before opening if it does not overlap usable Main Session check-in times; attendance scans themselves keep their existing time policy.

An admin creates one or more QR records for that day, each with a stable ID, descriptive name and initially `closed` status. A QR can be opened later or closed early with actor/time/reason recorded. More than one QR may be open concurrently: **opening QR-B does not close QR-A**. QR-A remains open for claims until the daily end time or until admin explicitly closes QR-A, subject to the normal eligibility, pause and stock checks. Opening a QR at 18:30 never changes a 19:00 day-end configured earlier. QR management is independent of the wheel's published prize configuration. The admin display view shows a large QR, name, status and Bangkok deadline. Preview of a closed QR must be clearly marked unavailable.

Whether admin may change the daily window after the first QR has opened is **not yet confirmed**. The system must not silently contradict an expiry already shown to participants. The policy for edits, extensions and credits already expired is an open decision below, not a fixed restriction here. Pause/resume, stock refill, opening additional QRs and closing an individual QR remain available within the confirmed flow.

QR payload is an HTTPS link to the existing localized PRIS app's dedicated claim page. Use a cryptographically random, non-sequential QR identifier, never the admission `regCode`, reward redemption token, PII or an incrementing database ID. Because the QR is projected publicly and shared, the identifier is **not** the authority to claim: authenticated ownership, registration, active attendance, QR/day state and time are checked server-side. Keep the QR identifier out of application analytics and request logs. The claim page should remove a query identifier from browser history after reading it where practical and use `Cache-Control: no-store` and a restrictive referrer policy. A human-readable manual code is a proposed fallback, not a confirmed QR field or storage/API requirement.

The QR link must work from the phone's ordinary camera and browser. It cannot depend on an in-LINE camera. If login/signup is needed, preserve only this validated internal claim destination and QR identifier through the existing localized redirect flow; reject arbitrary external redirect values. The fallback when a participant cannot scan needs the separate decision below. Opening a QR link after authentication triggers one claim POST automatically so the promised flow is “scan → receive one credit”; opening the page with GET alone never mutates data. The server's `(QR, account)` uniqueness makes duplicate page loads safe. Show the actual server result and QR name/day/deadline, rather than a premature success state.

## Claim and spend rules

For a new claim, the server derives account identity from the PRIS authentication token. It verifies the account is active, owns a confirmed registration for the same event, has the original Main Session entitlement, and has one active Main Session check-in for the QR's Bangkok date. It verifies the QR belongs to that event/date, is open, current server time is inside the single daily window, the wheel is published and unpaused, and at least one enabled physical-prize segment has stock. Existing no-prize segments alone cannot allow new claims. No client-supplied day, owner, attendance ID or QR state is trusted.

Create one durable credit claim for `(QR ID, user ID)` with a database unique constraint. Record claim time, QR/day/event, user ID and the active attendance ID used at claim. A duplicate tap, repeated scan or concurrent request returns the existing credit/status without creating another. If it was revoked or expired, return that state; never create a replacement from the same QR for that user. A retry after a lost response must reveal the original successful claim even if the QR later closes, the wheel pauses or the daily window ends. Failed validation does not consume the QR for that account.

When spinning, the server rechecks active account, confirmed registration, Main Session entitlement, active check-in on the current Bangkok date, the same daily window used for claims, publication/version, pause and physical stock. It finds one unspent, unrevoked credit for this event/account/date with `now < expiresAt`, choosing the earliest claimed/expiring credit deterministically. The client does not select a credit or outcome. One atomic database transaction locks the wheel and chosen credit, chooses an eligible segment with equal chance per eligible slot, decrements one real-prize unit when applicable, writes the spin, and marks exactly that credit spent. A no-prize result also spends one credit. If paused, out of physical stock, outside the daily window, attendance invalid or another validation fails, no credit or stock is spent. A repeated spin request key returns its original committed result before checking current conditions; a fresh request uses another credit.

Cancelling the check-in used at claim leaves the unspent credit recorded but blocks spinning. If the attendee obtains a new valid check-in on the same date before the daily deadline, the same credit becomes spendable again. Store the active attendance ID/time used by the successful spin as existing audit evidence; it can differ from claim-time attendance. Cancelling check-in after spin has no retroactive effect. Credits claimed on 29 October cannot be spent on 30 October, even if the Main Session covers both dates.

Closing one QR stops new claims from it and does not delete already claimed credits. Pausing the wheel or exhausting all physical prizes stops both new claims and new spins, leaving prior unused credits intact until their applicable daily expiry. Resume/refill before expiry makes them usable again if attendance is still valid. Expiry needs no reset cron: the API compares trusted server time with each credit's stored expiry. Treatment of expiry when admin edits an already opened day's window remains an open decision.

## Data and migration

Use an additive migration after the latest actual migration number, with corresponding Drizzle schema changes:

- `lucky_wheel_days`: event, Bangkok date, one start instant, one end instant and schedule audit/version. The post-open edit policy remains open.
- `lucky_wheel_qr_codes`: event/day FK, random opaque ID, display label, open/closed status, creator and open/close actor/time/reason. Unique random values and event/day constraints. Keep public display data separate from reward proof. Add manual-code fields only if that fallback is approved.
- `lucky_wheel_credit_claims`: QR FK, event/day, authenticated user, claim attendance FK, claimed time, applicable daily expiry, optional revocation actor/time/reason, optional spent time. Unique `(qr_id, user_id)` and status-consistency constraints. Track administrative corrections append-only or via an audit table with immutable before/after entries. How a later schedule change affects this expiry remains an open decision.
- `lucky_wheel_spins`: add nullable `credit_claim_id` FK with a unique index where non-null. Keep existing non-null `attendance_id` and `attendance_checked_in_at` because the **spin** still requires today's valid check-in. Drop the unrestricted `(event_id, user_id, play_date)` unique index; replace it with a partial legacy-only unique index where `credit_claim_id IS NULL` so old once-per-day rows retain their constraint while new credit-backed rows permit multiple same-day spins. Preserve historic rows with null credit claim ID. Do not fabricate credits for them. `play_date` remains populated from server Bangkok time.

The database uniqueness and transaction, not frontend button disabling, enforce one claim per account per QR and one spin per credit under concurrent use. Keep existing wheel-first lock ordering for spin, pause, stock and QR claim/open/close operations; agree a deterministic QR/claim/attendance order to avoid deadlocks. Validate event/QR/registration/attendance ownership in SQL before mutation. Preserve old spin history and reward/redemption foreign keys, collection status, prize snapshots and audit trails. No existing attendance history is removed.

## API contract direction

Follow the existing prefixes (`/api/backoffice/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId` and `/api/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId`), auth/error envelope, bounded validation, pagination and idempotency conventions. Exact DTO names can be finalized in the implementation plan without changing these behaviors.

| Endpoint direction | Role and effect |
| --- | --- |
| `PUT /api/backoffice/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/days/:date` | Admin configures the one Bangkok-day start/end window shared by claims and spins; compare version and audit changes. Whether an opened day's window may be edited is an open decision. |
| `GET /api/backoffice/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/days/:date` | Admin reads that day's schedule and current state; no mutation. |
| `POST /api/backoffice/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/qr-codes` | Admin creates a bounded batch of named, initially closed QRs for an existing day; idempotent batch retry. |
| `GET /api/backoffice/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/qr-codes` | Admin lists QR state/counts by day; paginated recipient/claim inspection. A separate admin detail/display response provides the official QR link and projection data. |
| `PATCH /api/backoffice/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/qr-codes/:qrId` | Admin explicitly opens/closes a QR with audit; cannot silently revoke existing claims. |
| `POST /api/backoffice/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/credit-claims/:claimId/revocations` | Admin revokes only an unspent individual credit with mandatory reason; append audit. A spent credit returns a conflict. |
| `GET /api/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/qr-codes/:qrId` | Authenticated attendee reads safe QR label/date/deadline/status for preview; no mutation or private recipient list. |
| `POST /api/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/credit-claims` | Authenticated attendee claims by QR ID after camera-link arrival; 201 new, 200 original for same `(QR, account)`, consistent 4xx for ineligible/closed/expired. |
| `GET /api/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/eligibility` | Own available credit count, expiry, current eligibility/block reason and current wheel state. Existing history/detail endpoints continue to serve all spins. |
| `POST /api/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/spins` | Existing idempotent spin route consumes one server-selected valid credit and returns the committed spin. |

Admin-only QR display uses the saved QR identifier and official PRIS URL; it must not expose reward redemption QR credentials. Reject arbitrary QR URLs. GET endpoints have no claim side effect. List endpoints paginate; admin recipient inspection is scoped to event. Audit who created/opened/closed a QR, who revoked a claim, why and when. Ordinary check-in staff cannot manage wheel QRs by virtue of their attendance assignment. A manual-code lookup API is optional and must be designed only if that fallback is approved.

## Screens and user feedback

Backoffice: add a focused QR rights section to the existing Lucky Wheel area rather than repurposing the check-in scanner. Admin selects day, sets one start/end window for both claiming and spinning, creates named QRs in a bounded batch, opens/closes chosen QRs, opens a full-screen projection view and sees claim/spent/revoked counts plus paginated recipient records. Close-QR and individual-revocation actions are visibly distinct; both require confirmation/reason appropriate to their effect. Show a clear warning if the planned daily window begins before the Main Session scan window. Opening another QR does not close those already open.

PRIS: add a mobile-first TH/EN claim page reached by the camera URL. Handle login return, automatic single claim after authenticated QR-link arrival, repeated claim, inactive QR, missing or cancelled check-in, wrong event, paused/out-of-stock, expired window and network uncertainty separately. Success says one credit was added and shows the exact Bangkok expiry, e.g. “ได้รับสิทธิ์หมุน 1 ครั้ง ใช้ได้ถึง 19:00 น. วันนี้ (เวลาไทย)” using real configured time. The wheel page shows available credits and expiry, stays usable for another intentional spin when credits remain, and shows the latest committed result without treating it as a daily terminal state. Existing history must support multiple spins on one day; prize proof/redemption remains as built. Preserve approved B brand, wheel geometry, fair-slot explanation, sold-out gray positions and accessibility basics.

The QR claim page must not depend on LINE in-app camera. The normal phone camera opens the official PRIS URL in a browser. If that browser has a separate auth store from LINE, request PRIS login there; the validated claim destination and QR identifier survive the return journey. Never show a false success when the server response is unknown. A non-camera fallback remains a decision to confirm, not a required manual-entry route.

## Verification and rollout

Automated checks cover: 100 distinct eligible accounts claiming one open shared QR (100 credits), 100 concurrent claims from the same account/QR (one credit), duplicate retries after lost responses, five QR codes giving five same-day spins, sixth new spin failing for lack of credit, many registrations for one account not multiplying claims, cross-event/other-user denial, closed/not-yet-open/expired QR, the same daily start/end boundary for claims and spins including Bangkok midnight, opening QR-B while QR-A stays claimable, paused/out-of-stock blocking both claim and spin, refill/resume before expiry, cancellation/re-check-in of claim-time attendance, cancellation after committed spin, claim revocation racing a spin, QR close racing a claim, final prize stock races and old history preservation. Real DB constraints are checked after concurrency tests; no request can produce negative stock, a duplicate `(QR, account)` claim or two spins from one credit. Schedule-edit tests depend on the post-open decision below.

Regression checks preserve ordinary attendance/session scans, date-filtered attendance reports, previous wheel spin history, reward QR/admin redemption, no-prize behavior, R2 imagery and approved B rendering. Test login return from ordinary mobile browsers and LINE launch, projection/normal-camera scan, slow network and device interruption in staging. Add fallback-specific tests only after its method is confirmed. Prior unverified real-device and staging-R2 checks remain explicit gates for field validation; do not claim local mocks prove them.

Deploy safely as an explicit reviewed operation: apply schema first; deploy compatible readers/API and PRIS/backoffice together while the wheel is paused; verify legacy spin rows and new constraints; configure the one shared Bangkok-day window and QRs before opening. No automatic QR opening, production sample codes, live grants, migration execution, push or deployment is implied by this design document. Printing or downloading a QR file is outside this confirmed scope.

## Open decisions for review

1. **Editing an opened day's window:** May admin change its start or end after any QR for that day opens? If yes, specify whether to allow extension, shortening or both, whether a credit whose prior deadline has passed can become valid again, and how updated expiry is shown/audited. A conservative proposal is to keep the start fixed after opening, allow audited end-time extensions only while the current window is still open and within the same Bangkok date, update unspent credits and the displayed deadline together, never revive credits after the previous end, and prohibit shortening after any claim. This is a proposal, not a confirmed rule.
2. **When camera scanning fails:** Is a human-readable code entered in PRIS required as a fallback, or should the initial release use another route? A manual code can resolve to the same QR and pass the same login, attendance, time and duplicate checks; it would require extra storage, lookup API, rate limiting, UI and tests. This is a proposal, not a confirmed requirement. Printing/downloading QR files is a separate unconfirmed capability and is not included in this design.
