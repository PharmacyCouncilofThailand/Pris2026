# PRIS2026 Lucky Wheel — Shared QR Credits Design

**Status:** User decisions incorporated on 2026-10-04; written design awaiting review. This replaces the spin-eligibility and once-per-day portions of `2026-10-03-lucky-wheel-design.md`. It keeps the approved daily Main Session check-in, wheel probability, stock, reward proof, redemption, R2 image storage, and mobile B composition unless a change is stated here. The old implementation plan is historical evidence and must not be executed as a change plan for this flow.

## Goal and fixed user decisions

Admin prepares multiple distinct shared QR codes for a Bangkok event date, initially closed, then opens chosen codes for projection on a large screen. A participant scans a projected QR with the phone's normal camera, follows its PRIS link, logs in if needed, and claims one wheel credit into their own account. Each `(event, QR, account)` can create at most one credit. The same QR can give one credit to every eligible account; five different QR codes can give one account five credits on the same day. There is no daily spin cap. The QR link is not a check-in or an admission QR.

Daily Main Session check-in remains independent and mandatory both when claiming a credit and when spending it. Staff continue scanning the original admission QR through the existing check-in workflow; staff never scan wheel-credit QRs on a participant's behalf. Admin alone manages the QR set, daily windows, opening/closing, claim records and corrections. Existing reward-redemption roles stay unchanged.

Each credit belongs to its QR's Bangkok date. Admin sets **one daily window** for both claiming QR credits and spinning; the start and end are identical for both actions. For example, QR claiming and spinning may both run 09:00–19:00 on one day, with different hours configured for another day. Admin may edit both times even after opening QRs. Unspent credits can be used only inside the current window on their own Bangkok date and never carry to the next date. A spent or admin-revoked credit cannot be restored by scanning the same QR again. A successful spin remains permanent when attendance is later cancelled; it does not restore credit or stock.

## Current implementation and required boundary

The completed API currently checks today's attendance and `spinByDay` inside `getEligibility` and `createSpin` (`conference-api/src/modules/lucky-wheel/service.ts`). `lucky_wheel_spins` requires attendance evidence and has a unique `(event_id, user_id, play_date)` index (`drizzle/0034_lucky_wheel.sql`). PRIS displays `existingSpin` as a terminal daily result (`Pris2026/src/app/[locale]/lucky-wheel/page.tsx` and `ActivityTicket.tsx`). The backoffice has wheel setup and reward collection but no QR-credit management.

Keep the existing daily attendance tables, policies, scan routes, ticket QR, reports, and historical check-in data. **Do not remove attendance checks from the wheel**: the new rule replaces today's single-play entitlement with a QR credit while retaining registration, Main Session entitlement and active daily attendance as preconditions. `play_date` remains a Bangkok reporting date; it ceases to be a uniqueness key for new spins.

## Day schedule and QR lifecycle

An admin creates one window for each `(event, Bangkok date)`, entering its start and end in `Asia/Bangkok`. Store absolute instants in PostgreSQL and the local date explicitly. The window must lie within that Bangkok date; an exclusive end at the following midnight is permitted. Both claiming and spinning use the **same current** `start <= databaseNow < end`, so a 19:00 end stops both at exactly 19:00. Validate `start < end`. Do not infer the window from the Main Session start/end. Show the configured window and warn before opening if it does not overlap usable Main Session check-in times; attendance scans themselves keep their existing time policy.

An admin creates one or more QR records for that day, each with a stable ID, descriptive name and initially `closed` status. A QR can be opened later or closed early with actor/time/reason recorded. More than one QR may be open concurrently: **opening QR-B does not close QR-A**. QR-A remains open for claims until admin explicitly closes it; actual claimability also depends on the current daily window, eligibility, pause and stock. Changing the window can stop or resume claims from any still-open QR without changing its open/closed status. Opening another QR does not itself change the day's schedule. QR management is independent of the wheel's published prize configuration. The admin display view shows a large QR, name, status and current Bangkok deadline. Preview of a closed QR must be clearly marked unavailable.

Admin can change both the start and end of that day's single window after QRs open, including extending or shortening either boundary. Require a reason, record the admin actor, server timestamp, old and new start/end and schedule version, and reject stale edits rather than overwriting a concurrent change. The change takes effect for **new claims and new spins together** after the schedule update commits; a claim/spin transaction racing the edit must see one complete schedule version. Previously committed spins, results, rewards and stock deductions are never changed by a later schedule edit. Pause/resume, stock refill, opening additional QRs and closing an individual QR remain independent controls.

Unspent credits follow the **current** window for their QR's date, rather than a permanently frozen deadline from the claim moment. Moving the start later temporarily blocks claims and use of existing credits until the new start. Moving the end earlier blocks both actions at the new end. If admin later extends the end within the same Bangkok date, an unspent, unrevoked credit can become spendable again while the new window is open, subject to today's active check-in, pause and stock; rescanning its QR never creates a second credit. Even a window ending at the next midnight cannot carry that credit to the next Bangkok date. This is the direct same-window interpretation of the approved free-edit rule; show the latest deadline from the server and make schedule changes visible to affected participants rather than continuing to display a stale promise.

QR payload is an HTTPS link to the existing localized PRIS app's dedicated claim page. Use a cryptographically random, non-sequential QR identifier, never the admission `regCode`, reward redemption token, PII or an incrementing database ID. Because the QR is projected publicly and shared, the identifier is **not** the authority to claim: authenticated ownership, registration, active attendance, QR/day state and time are checked server-side. Keep the QR identifier out of application analytics and request logs. The claim page should remove a query identifier from browser history after reading it where practical and use `Cache-Control: no-store` and a restrictive referrer policy. The first release has no manual claim code, code-entry field, code-lookup API or other fallback claim route.

The QR link must work from the phone's ordinary camera and browser. It cannot depend on an in-LINE camera. If login/signup is needed, preserve only this validated internal claim destination and QR identifier through the existing localized redirect flow; reject arbitrary external redirect values. Opening a QR link after authentication triggers one claim POST automatically so the promised flow is “scan → receive one credit”; opening the page with GET alone never mutates data. The server's `(QR, account)` uniqueness makes duplicate page loads safe. Show the actual server result and QR name/day/current deadline, rather than a premature success state.

## Claim and spend rules

For a new claim, the server derives account identity from the PRIS authentication token. It verifies the account is active, owns a confirmed registration for the same event, has the original Main Session entitlement, and has one active Main Session check-in for the QR's Bangkok date. It verifies the QR belongs to that event/date, is open, current server time is inside the single daily window, the wheel is published and unpaused, and at least one enabled physical-prize segment has stock. Existing no-prize segments alone cannot allow new claims. No client-supplied day, owner, attendance ID or QR state is trusted.

Create one durable credit claim for `(QR ID, user ID)` with a database unique constraint. Record claim time, QR/day/event, user ID, the active attendance ID used at claim and the deadline shown at claim time for audit. A duplicate tap, repeated scan or concurrent request returns the existing credit and its **current computed availability** without creating another. If it was revoked, spent or currently outside the window, return that state; never create a replacement from the same QR for that user. A retry after a lost response must reveal the original successful claim even if the QR later closes, the wheel pauses or the daily window changes or ends. Failed validation does not consume the QR for that account.

When spinning, the server rechecks active account, confirmed registration, Main Session entitlement, active check-in on the current Bangkok date, the **current shared daily window**, publication/version, pause and physical stock. It finds one unspent, unrevoked credit for this event/account/date while `start <= databaseNow < end` for that credit's day, choosing the earliest claimed credit deterministically. The client does not select a credit or outcome. One atomic database transaction locks the wheel, day schedule and chosen credit in a consistent order, chooses an eligible segment with equal chance per eligible slot, decrements one real-prize unit when applicable, writes the spin, and marks exactly that credit spent. A no-prize result also spends one credit. If paused, out of physical stock, outside the daily window, attendance invalid or another validation fails, no credit or stock is spent. A repeated spin request key returns its original committed result before checking current conditions; a fresh request uses another credit.

Cancelling the check-in used at claim leaves the unspent credit recorded but blocks spinning. If the attendee obtains a new valid check-in on the same date while the current daily window is open, the same credit becomes spendable again. Store the active attendance ID/time used by the successful spin as existing audit evidence; it can differ from claim-time attendance. Cancelling check-in after spin has no retroactive effect. Credits claimed on 29 October cannot be spent on 30 October, even if the Main Session covers both dates.

Closing one QR stops new claims from it and does not delete already claimed credits. Pausing the wheel or exhausting all physical prizes stops both new claims and new spins, leaving prior unused credits intact. Resume/refill makes them usable when the **current** window for their date is open and attendance is still valid. No reset cron is needed: the API compares trusted server time and current Bangkok date with the current day schedule. After that date ends, unused credits cannot carry forward or be revived on another date.

## Data and migration

Use an additive migration after the latest actual migration number, with corresponding Drizzle schema changes:

- `lucky_wheel_days`: event, Bangkok date, one current start instant, one current end instant and a version for concurrent edits. Persist append-only schedule changes with actor, reason, server time and old/new start/end. Edits are allowed after QRs open; do not store a second independent claim or spin window.
- `lucky_wheel_qr_codes`: event/day FK, random opaque ID, display label, open/closed status, creator and open/close actor/time/reason. Unique random values and event/day constraints. Keep public display data separate from reward proof. Do not add manual claim-code fields in the first release.
- `lucky_wheel_credit_claims`: QR FK, event/day, authenticated user, claim attendance FK, claimed time, claim-time displayed deadline for audit, optional revocation actor/time/reason and optional spent time. Unique `(qr_id, user_id)` and status-consistency constraints. Current availability/expiry is derived from the day schedule, not a frozen claim-time deadline. Track administrative corrections append-only or via an audit table with immutable before/after entries.
- `lucky_wheel_spins`: add nullable `credit_claim_id` FK with a unique index where non-null. Keep existing non-null `attendance_id` and `attendance_checked_in_at` because the **spin** still requires today's valid check-in. Drop the unrestricted `(event_id, user_id, play_date)` unique index; replace it with a partial legacy-only unique index where `credit_claim_id IS NULL` so old once-per-day rows retain their constraint while new credit-backed rows permit multiple same-day spins. Preserve historic rows with null credit claim ID. Do not fabricate credits for them. `play_date` remains populated from server Bangkok time.

The database uniqueness and transaction, not frontend button disabling, enforce one claim per account per QR and one spin per credit under concurrent use. Keep existing wheel-first lock ordering for spin, pause, stock, schedule edits and QR claim/open/close operations; lock the day schedule before reading its current bounds for claim/spin decisions, then use a deterministic QR/claim/attendance order to avoid deadlocks. Validate event/QR/registration/attendance ownership in SQL before mutation. Preserve old spin history and reward/redemption foreign keys, collection status, prize snapshots and audit trails. No existing attendance history is removed.

## API contract direction

Follow the existing prefixes (`/api/backoffice/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId` and `/api/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId`), auth/error envelope, bounded validation, pagination and idempotency conventions. Exact DTO names can be finalized in the implementation plan without changing these behaviors.

| Endpoint direction | Role and effect |
| --- | --- |
| `PUT /api/backoffice/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/days/:date` | Admin creates or edits the one Bangkok-day start/end window shared by claims and spins, including after QRs open. An edit requires a reason and expected version; commit new bounds and old/new audit together, or return a version conflict. |
| `GET /api/backoffice/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/days/:date` | Admin reads that day's schedule and current state; no mutation. |
| `GET /api/backoffice/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/days/:date/changes` | Admin reads paginated schedule-change audit with actor, reason, time and old/new bounds; no mutation. |
| `POST /api/backoffice/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/qr-codes` | Admin creates a bounded batch of named, initially closed QRs for an existing day; idempotent batch retry. |
| `GET /api/backoffice/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/qr-codes` | Admin lists QR state/counts by day; paginated recipient/claim inspection. A separate admin detail/display response provides the official QR link and projection data. |
| `PATCH /api/backoffice/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/qr-codes/:qrId` | Admin explicitly opens/closes a QR with audit; cannot silently revoke existing claims. |
| `POST /api/backoffice/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/credit-claims/:claimId/revocations` | Admin revokes only an unspent individual credit with mandatory reason; append audit. A spent credit returns a conflict. |
| `GET /api/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/qr-codes/:qrId` | Authenticated attendee reads safe QR label/date/current deadline/status for preview; no mutation or private recipient list. |
| `POST /api/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/credit-claims` | Authenticated attendee claims by QR ID after camera-link arrival; 201 new, 200 original for same `(QR, account)` with its current computed state, consistent 4xx for a new ineligible/closed/out-of-window claim. |
| `GET /api/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/eligibility` | Own unspent and currently spendable credit counts, current daily deadline and schedule version, eligibility/block reason and wheel state. Existing history/detail endpoints continue to serve all spins. |
| `POST /api/lucky-wheel/events/:eventId/spins` | Existing idempotent spin route consumes one server-selected valid credit and returns the committed spin. |

Admin-only QR display uses the saved QR identifier and official PRIS URL; it must not expose reward redemption QR credentials. Reject arbitrary QR URLs. GET endpoints have no claim side effect. List endpoints paginate; admin recipient inspection is scoped to event. Audit who created/opened/closed a QR, who revoked a claim, why and when. Ordinary check-in staff cannot manage wheel QRs by virtue of their attendance assignment. The first release has no manual-code lookup or alternate claim endpoint.

## Screens and user feedback

Backoffice: add a focused QR rights section to the existing Lucky Wheel area rather than repurposing the check-in scanner. Admin selects day, sets one start/end window for both claiming and spinning, creates named QRs in a bounded batch, opens/closes chosen QRs, opens a full-screen projection view and sees claim/spent/revoked counts plus paginated recipient records. Admin may edit both times after opening a QR; the edit form shows current bounds and requires a reason, confirms the effect on unspent credits, and shows the old/new audit history. The projection view reads the latest deadline rather than retaining a stale one. Close-QR and individual-revocation actions are visibly distinct; both require confirmation/reason appropriate to their effect. Show a clear warning if the planned daily window begins before the Main Session scan window. Opening another QR does not close those already open.

PRIS: add a mobile-first TH/EN claim page reached by the camera URL. Handle login return, automatic single claim after authenticated QR-link arrival, repeated claim, inactive QR, missing or cancelled check-in, wrong event, paused/out-of-stock, current-window closure and network uncertainty separately. A new claim says one credit was added and shows the **current** Bangkok use-by time, e.g. “ได้รับสิทธิ์หมุน 1 ครั้ง ใช้ได้ถึง 19:00 น. วันนี้ (เวลาไทย)” using real configured time. When admin changes the window, refresh the displayed deadline and credit availability from the server on page return/refocus and before a new spin; explain when the displayed deadline has changed. The wheel page distinguishes credits still held from credits currently spendable, shows the current deadline, stays usable for another intentional spin when credits remain spendable, and shows the latest committed result without treating it as a daily terminal state. Existing history must support multiple spins on one day; prize proof/redemption remains as built. Preserve approved B brand, wheel geometry, fair-slot explanation, sold-out gray positions and accessibility basics.

The QR claim page must not depend on LINE in-app camera. The normal phone camera opens the official PRIS URL in a browser. If that browser has a separate auth store from LINE, request PRIS login there; the validated claim destination and QR identifier survive the return journey. Never show a false success when the server response is unknown. The first release offers no manual code, entry form, lookup API or alternate claim route.

## Verification and rollout

Automated checks cover: 100 distinct eligible accounts claiming one open shared QR (100 credits), 100 concurrent claims from the same account/QR (one credit), duplicate retries after lost responses, five QR codes giving five same-day spins, sixth new spin failing for lack of credit, many registrations for one account not multiplying claims, cross-event/other-user denial, closed/not-yet-open/out-of-window QR, the same daily start/end boundary for claims and spins including Bangkok midnight, opening QR-B while QR-A stays claimable, paused/out-of-stock blocking both claim and spin, refill/resume while the current window is open, cancellation/re-check-in of claim-time attendance, cancellation after committed spin, claim revocation racing a spin, QR close racing a claim, final prize stock races and old history preservation. Test post-open start/end edits, shortening and extension, reopening an unspent credit within the same date, stale admin version conflicts, and schedule-edit races with claim/spin transactions. A committed spin/result must remain unchanged. Real DB constraints are checked after concurrency tests; no request can produce negative stock, a duplicate `(QR, account)` claim or two spins from one credit.

Regression checks preserve ordinary attendance/session scans, date-filtered attendance reports, previous wheel spin history, reward QR/admin redemption, no-prize behavior, R2 imagery and approved B rendering. Test login return from ordinary mobile browsers and LINE launch, projection/normal-camera scan, live schedule edits and displayed deadlines, slow network and device interruption in staging. The first release has no fallback-specific UI/API to test. Prior unverified real-device and staging-R2 checks remain explicit gates for field validation; do not claim local mocks prove them.

Deploy safely as an explicit reviewed operation: apply schema first; deploy compatible readers/API and PRIS/backoffice together while the wheel is paused; verify legacy spin rows and new constraints; configure the one shared Bangkok-day window and QRs before opening. No automatic QR opening, production sample codes, live grants, migration execution, push or deployment is implied by this design document. Printing or downloading a QR file is outside this confirmed scope.
